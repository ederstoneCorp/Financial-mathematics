Academic scope

Scope, source and content policy

Primary reference pack

The supplied 800-page PDF is an ActEd Combined Materials Pack for Subject CT1 (2013): Study Guide, Course Notes, Question and Answer Bank and assignments. It is a supporting reference, not the JKUAT syllabus.

How the pack is filtered

For the current build, relevant foundational material is drawn from the CT1 cashflow, time-value, interest-rate, discounting, annuity and loan chapters, with security valuation material treated selectively where it supports the working course scope.

  • Chapter 1: Cashflow models.
  • Chapter 2: The time value of money.
  • Chapter 3: Interest rates.
  • Chapter 4: Real and money interest rates.
  • Chapter 5: Discounting and accumulating.
  • Chapters 6–7: Annuities and varying annuities.
  • Chapter 8: Equations of value.
  • Chapter 9: Loan schedules.
  • Chapter 10: Project appraisal, only where included in the course scope.
  • Chapter 11–12: Investment securities and elementary compound-interest valuation, selectively.
The working topic list is based on the SMA 2207 scope previously discussed. The PDF itself is an actuarial CT1 pack and does not establish JKUAT examinability. Any topic not confirmed by the JKUAT course outline should be treated as supplementary until verified.

Copyright and originality

This platform uses original explanations, newly written examples and independently implemented calculators. It does not reproduce the ActEd pack or its question bank verbatim.

Academic accuracy

Check the assumptions on every problem: payment timing, compounding frequency, rate basis, number of periods and valuation date. A correct formula applied with inconsistent units gives a wrong answer.
